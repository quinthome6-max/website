# Quint Home: outreach pitch templates

Five templates for email or Instagram DM. Fill every [bracket] before sending, and open each one with a detail that shows you read the person's work. Keep the tone calm and exact: no exclamation marks, no superlatives, no "game-changer" language. Send nothing until Quint Home has approved the list and the sample budget (see `outreach-plan.md`).

Ground rules for every message:

- Facts must match the site: Monolith is cordless and covers up to 1,075 sq ft; oils are ₹899 (Hotel Credential oils ₹1,099); candles are ₹1,699; the Monolith, Pillar, Ember and Loom come with a complimentary 50 ml oil.
- Don't name hotels or say our oils copy a hotel's scent. Describe Grand Lobby and The Arrival as "composed in the spirit of a grand hotel lobby".
- Never make a link a condition of a free sample, and never ask for a "dofollow" link. Ask for a credit or a link and leave the link type to them. Gifted creator posts must be marked as gifted under ASCI rules.
- `/journal/corporate-gifts-india` is live (since 16 Nov 2026): link it in corporate-gifting pitches, with `/gifting` for personal gifting.
