# Quint Home: outreach plan (16 Nov 2026 – 10 Jan 2027)

**Goal:** get Indian sites to mention and link to Quint Home's guides and range pages, so the pages rank for searches other than the brand name. Targets are in `outreach-targets.csv` (45 rows) and messages in `pitch-templates.md`.

**Before anything is sent:** Quint Home approves (1) the target list, (2) the sample budget below, (3) who signs the emails (founder or team) and (4) the client list for "Scented by Quint Home" credits. Nothing has been sent.

## Order of outreach

| Week | Who | Why now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (16–22 Nov) | Self-serve listings: Justdial, IndiaMART. P1 gifting editors: Curly Tales, ELLE India, Harper's Bazaar India | Christmas and year-end gift guides are written in late Nov to early Dec |
| 2 (23–29 Nov) | P1 interiors and hotel-scent editors: Free Press Journal, ELLE DECOR India, Outlook Traveller, The Nod Mag. Existing hotel clients (credit request) | These titles already ran hotel-scent or home-scent pieces in 2026 |
| 3–4 (30 Nov–13 Dec) | P1 designers and creators: Essajees Atelier, The Orange Lane, The Purple Ink Studio, Rohina Anand Khira, Ridhi Khosla Jalan | Styled shoots take 3–6 weeks to publish |
| 5–6 (14–27 Dec) | P2: Esquire, LuxeBook, Deccan Herald, AD India, LBB, Housing.com, InstaCuppa, WinnerWinnerChickenDinner, remaining studios and creators, office and retail clients | Slots open as P1 replies come in |
| 7–8 (28 Dec–10 Jan) | P3 only if capacity remains; ask anyone who mentioned us without a link to add one | Mop-up |

Send at most 8–10 new pitches a week, so each one gets a personal first line and follow-ups don't slip.

## Follow-up

- Send one follow-up 7 days after the first message: two lines in the same thread, adding one new thing such as a photo, a date or a price point.
- If there's no reply after that, mark the target "closed – no reply" and leave it for at least three months.
- When someone replies yes, ship the sample within 3 working days and confirm the tracking number.
- Two weeks after delivery, check whether they've published. Ask once whether they need anything else.

## What to track (one row per target; a shared sheet is enough)

Target · contact route used · date sent · follow-up date · replied (Y/N, date) · outcome (yes / no / later) · sample shipped (item, value, date, AWB) · published (Y/N, date) · **published URL** · **link URL it points to** · link type (**followed / nofollow / sponsored / no link**) · notes. Add each live mention to `docs/seo/CHANGELOG.md`, and check Search Console → Links monthly.

## Sample budget (for approval)

Send samples only after a "yes", never with a cold pitch.

| Kit | Contents | Retail value | Suggested quantity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Editor kit | 1 candle + 1 Hotel Credential oil | ₹2,798 | up to 8 |
| Styling kit (designers, creators) | 1 diffuser + 2 oils | ₹5,800–₹21,200, by model | up to 5 |
| Oil-only kit (reviewers, smaller creators) | 2 oils | ₹1,798–₹2,198 | up to 6 |

If every kit is used, the retail value runs from about ₹65,000 (styling kits built on the ₹3,999 diffuser) to about ₹1.4 lakh (all five built on the ₹18,999 model). Diffusers sent to designers can be loans, returned after the shoot.

## Realistic target for 8 weeks

- **5–10 new mentions** on Indian sites. Of those, expect **2–5 to carry a link**: gifting edits often link to the product, and creator posts usually carry only a tag.
- **3+ listings live** (Justdial, IndiaMART, plus Google Business Profile from the SEO plan).
- **1–2 "Scented by Quint Home" client credits**, which are the most relevant links for `/businesses`.
- Most editorial links will be nofollow or sponsored, and that's normal. Never ask for a followed link in exchange for a product: Google treats that as a paid link. Mentions still build brand searches and the citations AI assistants draw on.
