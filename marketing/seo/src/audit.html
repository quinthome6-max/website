Five things to fix first
  1. Policy pages tell Google they are the homepage. /shipping, /privacy and /terms all carry a canonical tag pointing to the homepage.
  2. There is no Google Analytics, and no Search Console or Bing verification on the site.
  3. The business enquiry form does not submit. Every B2B lead from /businesses is lost.
  4. The homepage is slow on phones. A 4.5 MB hero video is the largest element; mobile content appears after 12.6 s in the lab test.
  5. There are no category pages for diffusers, oils or candles. Nothing targets "waterless diffuser" or "fragrance oil".
What is already right
  • Every page is fully server-rendered. Googlebot sees the same HTML as visitors.
  • The sitemap lists only live URLs (all 32 return 200) and includes 48 product images.
  • robots.txt keeps cart, checkout and admin out, and deliberately welcomes AI crawlers.
  • A good llms.txt already exists, with prices, links and key facts.
  • One H1 per page, clean readable URLs, real 404s, and 301 redirects for old product URLs.
  • Product pages carry Product and Breadcrumb schema with live Shopify prices.

Quint Home sells hotel-grade home fragrance from Mumbai at the top of the Indian market: diffusers from ₹3,999 to ₹18,999, above every product in the competitor roundups we found.

Brand-name risk

05 · Technical SEO

A strong foundation with one serious canonical bug and a heavy homepage

Crawling, rendering and redirects are in good shape. Three things need urgent work: a site-wide default canonical that points pages at the homepage, product schema that misses Google's shopping fields, and a homepage that is slow on phones.

What slows the mobile homepage

  • The hero video (hero-3.mp4, 4.5 MB) is set to preload fully, has no poster image, and is the largest element on screen. The hero still images are lazy-loaded, and nothing on the page has high fetch priority.
  • The Meta Pixel blocks the main thread for about 1.1 s on mobile, and Microsoft Clarity for about 0.3 s. Both load on every page.
  • Total page weight is 6.3 MB. Lighthouse flags about 98 KB of unused JavaScript and legacy JavaScript polyfills.
  • Images, videos and icons are never cached by browsers (max-age=0). The image optimiser cache lasts only 60 seconds, and only WebP is served, not the smaller AVIF.
  • Two unused videos (hero-deep.mp4 at 6.2 MB and hero-5.mp4 at 4.6 MB) are still deployed.
  • Pages regenerate in a US-East server region, even though the store serves only India.

07 · AI search readiness

Ahead of most Indian competitors, with small factual errors to fix

Quint Home already does what most brands haven't: it welcomes AI crawlers by name and publishes llms.txt files, so ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google's AI answers can read the catalogue. The gaps are factual consistency and too few answer-style pages.
