What this research could and couldn't see

We read every competitor's sitemap, homepage, main collection pages and a sample of their articles on 2 November. We could not see their Google rankings or search volumes: Semrush has no API units on our account, and search tools return US results. Pages in this plan are therefore ranked by buying intent and by how many competitors already target a phrase, which is a reliable sign of demand.

01

Almost all of Quint Home's Google traffic comes from people who already know the name. To grow, the site has to appear for what buyers type before they know us: "best aroma diffuser India", "diffuser for large room", "hotel fragrance oil", "corporate gifts under ₹5,000".

03

What each competitor does

Azhara: the one to watch

  • Same territory as Quint Home: homepage headline "Make Your Home Smell Like A 5-Star Hotel"; waterless range from the ₹6,999 Plume to the ₹25,499 Stelo XL; free oil with every diffuser.
  • 54 guides published in one batch in April, e.g. "What Size Diffuser Do You Need? Room Size Calculator", "Best Fragrance Oils for Diffusers in India", "Agarbatti vs Diffuser", "Best Diffuser for Bedroom", "How Luxury Hotels Scent Lobbies".
  • 1,549 automatically generated "[designer perfume] fragrance oil" pages and product names such as "Bleu de Chanel Inspired". Large reach, real trademark risk (section 10).
  • Sells through links inside the text, a cost table and related-post blocks, but no product cards.
  • A business-scenting page with a 5-step enquiry form, "GST invoice available" and "machines from ₹8,499".

The Aroma Aisle: buying-intent articles

  • All 32 posts target a purchase with "India" in the title: large rooms and 3 BHK, office reception, luxury cars, housewarming gifts under ₹10,000, corporate gifts under ₹5,000, plug-in diffusers for bathrooms, safety for babies.
  • Weaknesses we can beat: its main product link returns a 404 error, and it uses no FAQ markup.

SOSA: volume over quality

  • 41,021 posts, many plainly machine-written, with repeated URLs and product blocks unrelated to the post. Around 1,390 mention hotels and 34 name hotel chains ("Taj hotel fragrance for home").
  • The useful idea: a product card with price and stars at the top of every post. We already do this better, with live prices.

InstaCuppa: the "best in India" list

  • "Best Aroma Diffuser for Home in India (2026)" comes up first for "best waterless diffuser India" in our checks. It compares named brands in tables, dates its price checks, carries a founder byline and lists sources.
  • Our new buying guide targets the same search, from the brand that makes the product.

Isak, House of Aroma, Nyne and Fruik

  • Isak writes how-to and scent-marketing pieces and owns "first rain" for perfume. Nobody targets "first rain" or petrichor for diffuser oil: an opening for our First Rain.
  • House of Aroma wins on gifting categories (housewarming, wedding, corporate, Diwali hampers).
  • Nyne and Fruik compete on product, not on search: no articles, few page titles.
  • Name clashes: Azhara sells a "Drift" fragrance oil, so we title ours "Drift Car Diffuser"; Isak sells "First Rain" perfumes.

07

How every page sells

The template
  • The answer in the first two sentences, for Google's snippets and AI answers
  • A product card before the first heading, then one at each decision point ("for rooms over 600 sq ft…")
  • Live price, discount, "Free 50 ml oil" badge beside the text, Shop now; sold-out items say so
  • Question headings, an FAQ at the end, with FAQ markup
  • 3–5 links to related guides, one category page and the scent finder
What makes us more credible than them
  • Facts from our own product data: coverage, bottle sizes, prices that update with the shop
  • No invented comparisons, no other brands' trademarks
  • India-specific detail: BHK sizes, monsoon, AC placement, Mumbai flats
  • Recommended: founder byline and a short "about the author" line
  • Recommended: a GA4 event on product-card clicks, to see which guides sell

Getting mentioned: off-page plan

Roundups and press
  • Pitch Indian lifestyle and interiors titles for "best diffusers", "luxury home fragrance" and gifting roundups, with a sample
  • Hotel-feel story for interiors editors: the Monolith in a styled living room
  • Credit links from interior designers, architects and business clients who use Quint Home
Forums and listings
  • Many searches end in "reddit", and AI tools quote those threads. Answer real questions on Reddit and Quora as Quint Home, saying who we are; no fake accounts
  • Google Merchant Center free listings, using the product feed already built
  • Google Business Profile with photos and the Mumbai address
  • Submit every new page through IndexNow on the day it goes live
10

What we won't copy, and why

TacticWho does itWhy not
Designer "inspired by" or "dupe" oilsAzhara (1,549 pages), SOSAUses perfume houses' trademarks to win searches; a legal risk under India's Trade Marks Act, and ad platforms let owners block it. It also undercuts "scent that was designed". Take legal advice before using any brand name.
Hotel chain names in titlesSOSA (34 pages), AzharaSame trademark risk. We write about the palace-hotel lobby feel around Grand Lobby and The Arrival instead.
Mass machine-made postsSOSA (41,021)Google's scaled-content spam policy targets exactly this; one penalty can sink the whole site.
City pages without a presenceSOSAThin pages with no local substance. Isak's city pages work because they list real stores.
Years in page addressesInstaCuppa ("-2027")The year goes in the title, which can change; the address stays the same so links keep their value.
